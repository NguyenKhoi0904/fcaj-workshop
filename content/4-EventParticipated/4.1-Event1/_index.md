# Summary Report: “FCAJ Community Day - June 2026”

**Time:** 9:00AM - 12:00PM, Saturday, 27/06/2026
**Location:** 36th Floor, Bitexco Financial Tower, 2 Hai Trieu Street, Saigon, Ho Chi Minh City 700000, Vietnam.  
**Role:** Attendee.
**Speakers:** <br>
- Steve Trần - CTO at CloudThinker
- Trung Vũ - CEO Revve AI
- Kiệt Trần - AI Engineer
- Hiếu Nghị - Renova Cloud
- Chị Bảo, anh Nguyên Nguyễn - Cloud Engineer, Cloud Kinetics VietNam
- Trường Trần - AI Solution Sales, Noventiq VietNam.
- Anh Đặng - Solution Sales, Noventiq VietNam.
- Toàn Nguyễn - AWS Security Builder.

##### Nội dung chính:
- Deep Response Engine: From Incident Detection to Automated Response
    - Challenges arising from the complexity of operating modern cloud infrastructure.
    - Transitioning from alert-driven systems to action-driven systems.
    - Overview of the Deep Response Engine architecture.
    - Live demonstration of automated incident response and resolution capabilities.
    - Business impact: reducing costs and moving toward zero-downtime operations.
    - **Summary:** The Deep Response Engine session focused on project execution and how to identify strategic customers in order to solve real-world business problems.
- Voice Agents: Building Natural Human-Like Conversational AI Agents at Scale
    - The evolution from IVR (Interactive Voice Response) systems and traditional chatbots to AI Voice Agents.
    - Key challenges: latency, accuracy, and natural communication.
    - Amazon Nova Sonic and the speech-to-speech foundation model approach.
    - System architecture: integrating telephony, real-time streaming, Amazon Bedrock, and MCP tools.
    - Enterprise use cases, best practices, and a live demonstration.
    - **Summary:** The Voice Agents session discussed how to build voice capabilities for AI systems. It focused on the challenges of processing Vietnamese as a low-resource language, as well as gender recognition, context, regional accents, and the deployment of solutions such as Voice Agents in the banking sector.
- AWS DevOps Agent: Your Always-Ready Operations Teammate
    - Overview of AWS DevOps Agent.
    - Reducing MTTD (Mean Time to Detect) and MTTR (Mean Time to Resolve) through AI-assisted operations.
    - Support for multi-cloud and hybrid cloud environments.
    - Bedrock AgentCore and multi-agent reasoning approaches.
    - Real-world use cases and a demonstration on Amazon ECS.
    - **Summary:** This session introduced the capabilities of AI in DevOps, such as automated incident investigation, root cause analysis, and system improvement recommendations, significantly reducing MTTR (incident resolution time).
- Boosting Productivity with AI: Workforce Planning for Enterprises
    - Challenges in transforming human resource operations in modern enterprises.
    - Overview of Amazon Q and its capabilities for human resource management.
    - Accelerating HR processes through automation.
    - Workforce analysis and data utilization to support decision-making.
    - Strategic workforce planning to support enterprise-level decisions.
    - **Summary:** The session discussed the application of Amazon Q in HR. The solution can automate CV evaluation, candidate screening, and workforce strategy development, helping businesses save time and recruitment costs.
- Building Private and Secure MCP Connections with Amazon Q
    - Introduction to Amazon Q as an AI assistant platform.
    - MCP (Model Context Protocol) and its role in expanding integration capabilities.
    - Security challenges when integrating MCP-based systems.
    - Configuring private connections through Amazon VPC for Amazon Q.
    - Demonstration and practical implementation experience.
    - **Summary:**The session discussed enterprise security when deploying Amazon Q in large-scale systems and how to securely connect internal data.

##### Key Takeaways:
After attending the event, I realized that AI and cloud computing are being widely applied to solve real-world problems across various fields, including DevOps, human resources, and enterprise operations. I also learned that deploying AI solutions requires not only technical knowledge but also a clear understanding of business needs, appropriate data preparation, and strong security measures when accessing internal data.

The event also demonstrated that AI is becoming an effective support tool for automating processes, improving productivity, and optimizing business operations.
