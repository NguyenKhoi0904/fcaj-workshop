Throughout my internship at **Amazon Web Services Vietnam Co., Ltd.**, from 22/06/2026 to 15/08/2026, I had the opportunity to learn, gain experience, and develop my skills in a professional technology environment. This was not only a period that helped me strengthen the professional knowledge I had acquired during my studies at university, but also provided an opportunity for me to gradually apply that knowledge to practical activities and tasks.

During the internship, I participated in the **AWS FCAJ Workforce Bootcamp 2026 (First Cloud AI Journey)** and had the opportunity to learn and practice with various technologies and services within the **Amazon Web Services (AWS)** ecosystem. In addition, I participated in building and developing the **RAG Knowledge Assistant** project, which allowed me to apply knowledge of **Cloud Computing**, **Artificial Intelligence (AI)**, **Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG)**, and related technologies to a real-world problem. Through this process, I gradually improved my professional skills such as programming, analysis and problem-solving, learning new technologies, and system deployment, while also developing soft skills such as teamwork, communication, time management, documentation, and report writing.

In addition to professional knowledge, I also focused on developing a serious and responsible working attitude. Throughout the internship, I consistently tried to complete assigned tasks on time, proactively research and solve problems that arose during the work process. At the same time, I followed the program's rules and regulations, maintained a learning-oriented mindset, actively communicated with team members, and accepted feedback to gradually improve the quality of my work. These experiences helped me better understand the expectations of working in the technology field, as well as the importance of responsibility, proactiveness, and self-learning ability in a real-world working environment.

Through the internship, I realized that I had made certain progress in professional knowledge, practical skills, and working attitude. At the same time, the process also helped me identify areas that still need improvement in the future.
